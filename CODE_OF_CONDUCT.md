# Code of Conduct

ZenithPS5 is a technical project, and its discussions should stay useful and kind.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear before concluding that it is wrong.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and accept it the same way.
- Keep to the topic: the code, the design, the tests, the documentation.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Pressure for software piracy, or for circumventing console protections (see the
  [legal policy](docs/legal/policy.md)).
- Spam, or repeated off-topic requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who do not follow these rules, with
or without warning depending on the severity. If you are the target of such behavior or see it, report it to
the maintainers through the repository (a private security advisory works for this too) and it will be dealt
with discreetly.
